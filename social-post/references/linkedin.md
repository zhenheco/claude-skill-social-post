# LinkedIn

> last_verified: 2026-11-03
> scope: durable writing, publishing and measurement workflow; this reference does not enable LinkedIn routing or data storage in Social Post.

## Drafting

- Choose a professional audience and one useful insight, question or experience. Make the premise clear in the opening, then support it with relevant detail.
- Use text, images, documents or video when they serve the message; do not treat any format, hashtag count or external link as a guaranteed reach formula.
- LinkedIn says both relevance and recency inform Feed ranking. Write for lasting professional value where appropriate, but do not promise that an older post will resurface or assign it a fixed lifespan. [LinkedIn relevance](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1339724) (verified: 2026-11-03).
- A Buffer-linked analysis examined approximately 2 million posts from LinkedIn profiles connected to Buffer; its analyst suggests 2–5 posts per week as a possible starting point. Within-account comparisons associated higher frequency with higher engagement rates but lower reach per post; Buffer's editorial summary reports the opposite direction for per-post reach. Treat the frequency as a hypothesis, not a guarantee or proof of causation, and test a sustainable cadence against your own baseline. [Original analysis](https://symphonious-shortbread-cd2770.netlify.app/posts/linkedin-posting-frequency/) · [Buffer summary](https://buffer.com/resources/how-often-to-post-on-linkedin/) (verified: 2026-11-03).

## Publishing

- Preview the final copy, media, audience, destination and active identity before sending. Live publication needs current-session confirmation.
- Record the final caption, format, publication time and post permalink separately until LinkedIn is supported by the Social Post data workflow.

## Measurement

Keep impressions, members reached, engagement, clicks and follows distinct. LinkedIn defines impressions as displays and members reached as members shown the post; preserve the UI labels and any rounding. [LinkedIn metric definitions](https://www.linkedin.com/help/recruiter/answer/a1624090) (verified: 2026-11-03).

Compare posts at similar maturity, with similar topics and formats. Record snapshot time and simultaneous changes in cadence or packaging; an observed difference does not establish its cause.
